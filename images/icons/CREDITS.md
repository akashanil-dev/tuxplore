# Icon credits

Each desktop environment uses its own icon theme, the way real Linux desktops do. Icons from other
projects are used unmodified under their licenses.

The official icons of GNOME apps, made by the GNOME Design Team and app authors (generally CC-BY-SA;
Impression is GPL-3.0).

## CDE: `retro/`

Drawn for TuxOS in the style of the 1990s Motif desktop: a CRT terminal, a filing cabinet, a notepad,
pipes, falling blocks, a trophy, the Style Manager palette and a floppy disk.
